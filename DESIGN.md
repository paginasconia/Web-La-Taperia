## Brand & Style

The brand personality captures the vibrant soul of an Andalusian neighborhood bar in Dos Hermanas: warm, hospitable, steeped in authentic culinary pride, yet crisp and uncluttered. It balances the comfort of a bustling tapas counter with contemporary digital craftsmanship.

The visual style blends **warm tactile minimalism** with **regional editorial elegance**:
- Sun-washed architectural surfaces evoke whitewashed facades and warm lime plaster.
- Deep terracotta and olive accents echo fired clay, seasoned cookware, and Andalusian olive groves.
- Subtle geometric motifs inspired by traditional Sevillian ceramic tiles (*azulejos*) serve as micro-accents and divider terminals without cluttering content.
- Generous breathing space and crisp layout architecture keep menus, daily specials, and contact actions immediately accessible.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the natural minerals, architecture, and culinary landscape of lower Andalusia:

- **Primary (`#C84B31` / Terracota Teja):** Used for focal action drivers, primary reservation buttons, active status badges, and prominent pricing callouts. For pressed or high-contrast states, step down to `#B33925`.
- **Secondary (`#5B7052` / Verde Oliva Andaluz):** Represents freshness, wine, cold-pressed olive oils, and secondary confirmations. Deep variant `#44593B` provides high-contrast borders and icon fills.
- **Tertiary (`#DCA152` / Albero Sevillano):** A warm ocher accent applied sparingly to highlight house recommendations, star ratings, specialty badges, and celebratory banners.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Base background canvas: `#FAF7F2` (Cal Cálida).
  - Raised card and dialog surface: `#F5EFEB` (Mortero Suave).
  - High-elevation surface (popovers, active sheets): `#FFFFFF`.
  - Body and headline text: `#2B2118` (Café Tostado) providing contrast without the harshness of pure black. Secondary captions use `#6B5E52`.
  - Structural hairline borders: `#E5DDD0` (Arcilla Pálida).

## Typography

The typography pairing unites classical Andalusian poise with modern readability:

- **Playfair Display** introduces heritage, warmth, and culinary authority in section headers, category titles, and signature dish highlights. It should be typeset with gentle optical kerning and standard italic emphasis for culinary terms or wine designations (*e.g., Manzanilla de Sanlúcar*).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers crisp, open legibility for dish descriptions, prices, allergen notations, and navigation labels. Its humanized geometric structure keeps long-form menus effortless to scan on mobile screens under direct sunlight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a fluid column system designed for mobile-first hospitality scenarios:

- **Grid Structure:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1rem), and `gutter-mobile` (0.75rem). Persistent tap-friendly bottom bar for quick call and location triggers.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, 1.5rem margins, 1rem gutters. Two-column menu presentation (tapa / media / ración).
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout centered at 1200px max width, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Component interiors utilize compact paddings (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure dense, easy-to-read menu items.
  - Generous section transitions use `space-xl` combined with decorative 1px warm borders (`#E5DDD0`) featuring centered terracotta diamond motifs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-altitude depth inspired by sunlit architectural relief rather than artificial drop shadows:

- **Tonal Tiers:** Surfaces stack with tint differences: Canvas (`#FAF7F2`) supports Base Cards (`#F5EFEB`), which elevate into Active / Floating Cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** When elevation is required (sticky call buttons, modals, dropdowns), shadows use a soft terracotta-tinted brown ambient cast rather than cold grays:
  - *Subtle (Menu cards, hover items):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.06), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(200, 75, 49, 0.04)`.
  - *Elevated (Sticky action bar, popovers):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.10), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(200, 75, 49, 0.06)`.
- **Low-Contrast Framing:** All cards and containers feature a delicate 1px border in `#E5DDD0` to anchor them neatly against the cream backdrop.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Rounded Level 2** organic geometry:
- Standard elements (inputs, interactive chips, menu dish rows) use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger containers, meal category highlights, and image cards employ `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Prominent call buttons and quick-action triggers use full pills (`9999px`) to create an inviting, tactile contrast that prompts taps.
- Decorative accent frames may subtly incorporate soft inverted corner notches on specialty banners to evoke classic ceramic tile silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call to Order / Reserve):** Solid terracotta (`#C84B31`) background with pure white text, pill shape, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. In hover/pressed states, shifts to `#B33925` with an organic 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary Action (Directions / Location):** Solid olive green (`#5B7052`) background with pure white text, paired with a subtle map pin icon.
- **Tertiary / Ghost (View Menu / Details):** Cream background (`#F5EFEB`), border in `#C84B31` at 1.5px, text in `#C84B31`, rounded 8px.

### Menu Cards & Tapas Listings
- Display dishes in a clean, horizontal row format. Left side: Dish title in `Playfair Display` (18px) with dietary/allergen icons in `#5B7052`. Right side: Distinct price tiers (Tapa / Media / Ración) typeset in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.
- Recommended dishes feature an Albero gold pill tag (`#DCA152` background with `#2B2118` text) reading "Sugerencia de la Casa".

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- Used for navigating categories (e.g., *Chacinas*, *Fritos*, *Guisos*, *Montaditos*, *Vinos*).
- Default: Border in `#E5DDD0`, surface `#FAF7F2`, text `#2B2118`.
- Selected: Background `#C84B31`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `#C84B31`.

### Inputs & Reservation Form Elements
- Inputs feature `#FFFFFF` fill on `#FAF7F2` backgrounds, framed with a 1px border in `#E5DDD0`.
- Active focus state replaces border with a 2px stroke of `#C84B31` and an outer halo of `rgba(200, 75, 49, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded 4px (checkboxes) and full circle (radios).
- In checked state: Filled with `#5B7052` containing a crisp white tick mark.

### Bottom Quick-Action Bar (Mobile Only)
- Fixed at the bottom viewport: high blur (`backdrop-blur-md`) with `#FAF7F2` at 92% opacity, separated by a 1px `#E5DDD0` top rule.
- Dual-button layout: One-touch direct dial (`Llamar al Bar`) in primary terracotta, paired with an address button (`Cómo llegar`) in secondary olive.
